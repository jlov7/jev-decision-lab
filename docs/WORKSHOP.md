# Workshop and demonstration script

## Five-minute leadership demonstration

**Minute 1 — the boundary.** Open S02. Say: “This is a synthetic teaching case. The model output is a decision sheet, not an essay, and the surrounding code still owns the route.” Run the six questions. Show a distribution and the difference between probability and the separate confidence statistic.

**Minute 2 — evidence beats confidence.** Mark the evidence expired. Replay policy. Point out zero new model calls: “The model did not get smarter. The code applied a current constraint to an old judgment.” Do not imply the stale-source toggle is a real system-of-record integration.

**Minute 3 — authority is separate.** Return to the original policy and recheck the simulated action. Remove current approval. The action is held: “A previous recommendation is not a current permission.” Zero external actions remains visible.

**Minute 4 — make the limitation visible.** Run S04 and show the deliberately confident wrong owner. Open evaluation. “These probabilities were authored to teach the failure mode; this is not a measured Jev error. A high number cannot validate itself.”

**Minute 5 — translate to the business.** Open Deliverable review. Explain the unsupported-guarantee and unapproved-scope cases. Then show Model garden: calculation goes to code, not a judgment model. Close with the experiment question: “Can this improve accepted decisions and reviewer effort against our strongest existing workflow?”

## Sixty-minute engineer session

Spend 10 minutes on the three primitives and request/response inspection. Spend 15 minutes on S02, S04, T03 and Q04, asking participants to predict the policy route before running. The workbench shows a case's teaching note only after it has been run, so the prediction is not spoiled. Spend 10 minutes distinguishing missing evidence, high consequence and expired authority. Spend 10 minutes on the source timeline and why “not in the brief” is not automatically an ingestion failure. Spend 10 minutes writing a rubric for one real workflow using synthetic examples. Spend 5 minutes agreeing a comparator, failure cost and a kill criterion.

An optional live call belongs after everyone understands replay provenance and after the account owner explicitly authorizes it. The workshop does not need live access to explain the architecture.

## Teach-back prompts

Ask each engineer to explain why a valid schema does not establish truth, why a Noul of 0.5 is not medium severity, why a Score mean can conceal a critical tail, why a more confident result can still be wrong, and what must happen when evidence changes after a model call.

Ask a domain owner which false negative would be most costly, what makes the current workflow a strong baseline, and how many extra reviews the team could absorb. Ask a platform reviewer which data crosses which provider boundary, where credentials live, and who can revoke an effect.

## What not to say during the demo

Do not say the fixtures are real Jev results, that this is “100% accurate,” that the hashes are signed proof, that an approval checkbox is a real authorization system, that a synthetic accuracy score is a production result, or that the model garden chooses the best current model. Do not claim the prelaunch timeline proves the tracker actually ingested those artifacts.
